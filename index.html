SIH Solution Concept • FoodTech & Rural Development

Stop food waste before it becomes waste.

ANNAPURNA 360 is a practical software-first platform that predicts food demand, detects potential surplus, guides safe next steps, connects suitable recipients and learns why waste happens.

How ANNAPURNA 360 works

1️⃣ PREVENT — Predict how much to prepare
2️⃣ RESCUE — Register and prioritize surplus
3️⃣ RECOVER — Choose an appropriate recovery pathway
4️⃣ LEARN — Build a Food Waste Fingerprint

Your Requirements — Built Into The Website

The project is designed around the requirements you gave for the hackathon.

01

Rural + Food Focus

Supports colleges, hostels, hotels, restaurants, caterers, supermarkets, institutional kitchens, food processors and Farmer/FPO workflows.

02

Low Budget

Software-first architecture. No expensive hardware is required for the MVP. Sensors can be added later.

03

One System, Multiple Problems

Prevention, surplus rescue, recipient matching, route planning, recovery and root-cause learning are connected in one workflow.

04

Practical + Explainable

Every decision shows the reason: demand estimate, surplus estimate, urgency, need, distance and recommended next action.

05

Innovation

The focus is not just donation. The system creates a Food Passport, Rescue Priority Score and Food Waste Fingerprint in one continuous loop.

06

Human-in-the-Loop Safety

AI is decision support, not a food-safety guarantee. Final redistribution decisions follow applicable rules and authorized personnel.

Basic Technical Architecture

Simple enough for an MVP, scalable for a real deployment.

Frontend

Responsive web app. Can later be converted into a Flutter mobile app.

Backend

Python + FastAPI for real APIs, authentication, matching and prediction services.

Database

PostgreSQL for users, food batches, partners, outcomes and historical data.

AI

Python, Pandas and Scikit-learn for demand forecasting, scoring and learning.

Maps

OpenStreetMap + routing service for practical low-cost route planning.

Offline-first

Local storage allows field users to record data and sync when connectivity returns.
